Our Story

Founded in 2010, Furni has grown from a small local car rental service to a premium luxury vehicle provider trusted by clients across the country.

Who We Are

At Furni, we believe every journey deserves the perfect vehicle. Our mission is to provide exceptional luxury and sports car rental experiences with unmatched service and attention to detail.

Starting with just three vehicles in our fleet, we've grown to offer over 50 premium cars from world-renowned manufacturers like Ferrari, Lamborghini, Mercedes-Benz, and more.

Our commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction has made us the preferred choice for discerning clients who expect nothing but the best.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Excellence

We strive for excellence in every aspect of our service, from the quality of our vehicles to the professionalism of our staff.

Integrity

We believe in transparency, honesty, and ethical business practices in all our dealings with customers and partners.

Passion

Our team shares a genuine passion for luxury automobiles and creating memorable experiences for our clients.
